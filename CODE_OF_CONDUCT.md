# Code of conduct

We want DocMax to be a welcoming place to learn and build. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews agrees to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback the same way.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Keep harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and unwelcome sexual attention out of the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, email punithmedaramitta@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.

This statement is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
